# Scene demo (60 seconds, hands-free)

Problem: you make promises all day in conversation and forget half of them.
Scene watches your day, understands the whole day at once, and makes sure
you follow through.

## The 60-second flow

1. Glasses on, recording. The live scene appears on the lens and phone.
2. Have a real conversation. Make a real promise (example: Maya asks you
   to take the trash out).
3. Pin the moment from the lens (pinch) or the phone.
4. Ask: "What did I promise Maya today?" Scene answers from the whole
   day in one Argon context, with verbatim quote receipts.
5. Follow-Through: the promise shows up as a verified draft. Approve it
   on the phone. Nothing sends without a tap.

## What to say while it runs

- "Scene doesn't retrieve chunks. It loads the entire day into one
  1M-token Argon context and reasons over it at once."
- "Scene doesn't trust its first draft. The Critic checks every scene
  against the stored transcript and keyframes."
- "Nothing sends without a tap."

## Backup

If the glasses stream fails, run the same flow on the phone with the
seeded day session. The dashboard is backup only, never the demo lead.
