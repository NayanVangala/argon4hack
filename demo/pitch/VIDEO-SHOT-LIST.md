# Scene - 60-Second Uncut Lens Video Shot List

Format: 60 seconds, uncut, no edits. One continuous take from the glasses view.
Purpose: demo video for submission. Shows what the glasses see, in order.

Rules for the whole take:
- No cuts. No edits. No splicing, no trimming around a failure, no B-roll inserted later.
- If a shot fails mid-take, restart the take from 0:00. Do not edit around it.
- If the take is restarted, say so on camera at the start of the new take if the restart is visible, and keep the failed take out of the submission only by replacing it with a full clean take, not by patching it.
- Narrate briefly while doing each gesture. Keep narration to what is happening on screen.

## Setup notes (before pressing record)

- Glasses charged. Confirm charge before the take, not during it.
- Charger on hand at the shooting location, in case charge drops between takes.
- Phone fallback rehearsed: if the glasses view cannot be shown, the phone view is the fallback, and the fallback is stated on camera, not hidden. The fallback take is still one uncut take, not a patchwork.
- Lens page host running and reachable before the take starts. Do not debug hosting on camera in the submitted take.
- A live scene ready to appear: either a genuinely live input or a clearly staged live input started just before the take, so the "live scene appearing" beat is real within the take, not a pre-recorded insert.
- Quiet background, one speaker for narration, no second person talking over the gestures.
- Test pin, timeline scroll, and back once before recording, then start a fresh take. The rehearsal is not the submission.

## Shot list, second by second (0:00-1:00, no cuts)

- 0:00-0:05 - Opening, glasses view live. Camera sees what the wearer sees. Narrate: "This is the Scene lens view, live, one uncut take."
- 0:05-0:12 - Pin. Pinch to pin the current moment/scene. Camera shows the pin landing. Narrate: "Pinch to pin."
- 0:12-0:25 - Timeline scroll. Swipe to scroll the timeline. Camera shows the timeline moving and scenes passing. Narrate: "Swipe to scroll the timeline."
- 0:25-0:32 - Back. Middle tap (back gesture) to go back. Camera shows the view returning. Narrate: "Tap to go back."
- 0:32-0:45 - Live scene appearing. A new scene appears in the lens view from live input during the take. Camera shows it arriving, not already sitting there from before the take. Narrate what the scene is, in one sentence.
- 0:45-0:55 - Hold on the new scene. Let the viewer read it. No new gesture. Narrate one line: what the scene captured and why it matters (a promise, a person, a decision).
- 0:55-1:00 - Close, still in the same take. Narrate: "Scene, live from the glasses. End of take." Stop recording.

Total: 60 seconds. If the take runs long, restart and tighten narration, do not trim the tail in editing.

## What to do if a shot fails mid-take

- Pin does not register: finish the sentence, stop recording, restart the take from 0:00. Do not re-shoot only the pin and splice it in.
- Timeline does not scroll, or scrolls the wrong way: stop recording, restart the take from 0:00.
- Back does not return: stop recording, restart the take from 0:00.
- No live scene appears by 0:45: stop recording, check the live input, restart the take from 0:00. Do not hold on an empty view and cut in a scene later.
- Glasses lose charge or connection mid-take: stop recording, charge/reconnect, restart the take from 0:00. If glasses cannot be recovered, shoot the phone-fallback take as one full uncut take and label it as the phone fallback on camera.
- Audio drops or narration is inaudible: stop recording, restart the take from 0:00. Do not dub audio over it later.

## Checklist before submitting the video

- [ ] One file, one continuous take, 60 seconds, no cuts or edits detectable.
- [ ] Pin, timeline scroll, back, and a live scene appearing all visible, in that order.
- [ ] No dashboard as the lead view. The lens view is the video.
- [ ] No feature shown that was not built during the hackathon.
- [ ] If the phone fallback was used, it is stated on camera in the take itself.
